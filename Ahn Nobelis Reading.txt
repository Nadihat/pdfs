Ahn Nobelis 10-Minute Reading

I ask them "Are you ready to do the reading." They say "Gung-ho-yes."

I feel a little nervous about getting this right and reading them accurately.

I see Crash Bandicoot holding a soccer ball and spinning it on his finger.

A bicycle in a garage.

Walking a bicycle on a street.

"The path to the motion is in the stars. Unending. Decisive. Victory. Unleashed. Forever."

A river.

The word "wallet".

A marshmallow.

A kayak/inflatable raft on rapids.

An yellow anthro smoking a cigar around a campfire with friends at night.

A feeling of stillness, memories, living, everlasting, ever-living memory.

"The past is now."

"No striving."

Green grass in need of mowing in a strip by a sidewalk.

A building with cardboard over the windows.

A garage door opening to a view of the sky from inside.

Fishing gear that is unused.

An anthro using a fishing rod and smoking a cigar.

A human hand.

A plane in the sky.

I ask what they think of a higher power. "Circular reasoning. It is endless. Circumstances prevail. Into the night. It is endless. Again, tragic yet dignified. They know all too well the importance of letting go. It is well."

A bowling ball, green with a wavy pattern.

An anthro giraffe with chalk pointing at a chalkboard teaching a class.

A timeless focus into the moment. Energy focuses inward.

"It is all out there, waiting to be discovered. Let it come to you. Bring what you seek, to you, inward, forever expanding into bliss and insight. It is well. It is love. You are loved. We are One. We are United. It is standing, fresh, ever present, into a naked now. Be naked with yourself, and ok with that. Be vulnerable, into the twilight of dawn, it seems.

We relinquish this connection.

It has been a delight in connecting with you dear one."
